1. Modes of Communication

A communication mode tells us how data moves between two devices—one way, both ways alternately, or both ways at the same time.

Perfect definition: Communication mode is the direction and timing in which data is transmitted between communicating devices.

Simplex
Data moves in only one direction.
Examples: TV, radio, keyboard.
Half-duplex
Both can send, but only one at a time.
Examples: Walkie-talkie, CB radio.
Full-duplex
Both can send and receive simultaneously.
Examples: Phone call, video call.

3. Types of Communication Channel / Media

A communication channel or transmission medium is the path through which a signal travels from source to destination.

COMMUNICATION CHANNEL / MEDIA

🔌 Wired / Guided Media

Signals travel through a physical path such as copper wire or glass fibre.

Twisted PairCAT 6Optical Fibre

📡 Wireless / Unguided Media

Signals travel through air, space or vacuum as electromagnetic waves.

Wi‑FiBluetoothRFIDSatellite
Quick trick: Guided = Given path (wire guides the signal). Unguided = Uses air.

7. Why Are the Wires Twisted?

Increase the number of twists. Observe how the conceptual interference level falls.

Estimated interference: 58%

Reason: Twisting makes external electromagnetic noise affect both wires more equally, helping the receiver cancel unwanted noise.

Use: Telephone lines and Ethernet LANs.

Exam sentence: “The twisting of wire pairs reduces electromagnetic interference and crosstalk, improving signal quality.”

Trick: “More twist, less disturbance.”
