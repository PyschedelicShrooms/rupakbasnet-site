10. Common mistakes

Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌

“Star has no central device.”

Wrong. A star depends on a central hub or switch.

❌

“Ring sends everywhere at once.”

In the basic model, data follows the ring path, commonly one direction.

❌

“Hybrid means wireless.”

Hybrid means a combination of two or more topology types.

✅

Remember the weak point

Bus: backbone. Star: center. Ring: loop break. Hybrid: depends on its design.

11. Quick revision

One-Screen Summary

Bus

One shared backbone. Cheap and simple, but the backbone is critical.

Star

All devices connect to a hub/switch. Easy to manage; center is critical.

Ring

Devices form a loop. Organized flow; a break can disrupt a basic ring.

Hybrid

Mixes topology types. Flexible and scalable, but more complex and costly.
