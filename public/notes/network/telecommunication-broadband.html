Write in Copy

When a person speaks into a phone, the microphone receives the sound waves. The microphone converts the sound waves into electrical signals. The phone converts these electrical signals into digital signals made of 0s and 1s. These digital signals travel through a communication medium such as cable, radio wave, optical fiber or satellite. At the receiving end, the phone converts the signals back into sound, and the receiver hears the original message.

5. Broadband: Fast Internet Connection 🚀

Broadband is a high-speed, always-on Internet connection that can send and receive large amounts of data at the same time.

Write in Copy

Broadband is a fast Internet connection that remains continuously connected and allows users to upload and download data quickly. It is used for online classes, video calls, browsing, streaming, gaming and file sharing.

Why is broadband faster than dial-up? 🐢 vs 🚀

🐢
Dial-up Internet

Uses a telephone line in a slow way. It usually sends less data at one time and cannot use phone and Internet easily together.

🚀
Broadband Internet

Uses a wider data path. It can carry more data at the same time and stays connected continuously.

Write in Copy: Why Broadband is Faster

Broadband is faster than dial-up because it has a wider bandwidth. Bandwidth means the capacity of a connection to carry data. A wider bandwidth can carry more data at the same time, so web pages, videos and files load faster.

Write in Copy: How Broadband Works

Broadband works by sending Internet data from the ISP through a broadband line such as fiber, cable, DSL or wireless connection. The modem receives the Internet signal and the router shares it to devices using Wi‑Fi or cable. Because broadband has high bandwidth, it can send and receive more data quickly.

Write in Copy: Speed of Broadband Types

  1. DSL: Uses telephone lines and provides moderate speed.
  2. Cable Internet: Uses cable TV lines and is usually faster than DSL.
  3. Wireless Broadband: Uses radio signals without cables.
  4. Mobile Broadband: Uses 3G, 4G and 5G mobile networks.
  5. Satellite Internet: Useful in remote areas but may have more delay.
  6. Fiber-optic Internet: Uses light signals through glass fiber and is usually the fastest.

Why speed differs: Internet speed depends on bandwidth, technology, signal quality, distance, number of users and network traffic.
